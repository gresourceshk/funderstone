About us

FUNDERSTONE SECURITIES HOLDINGS LIMITED is a wholly-owned subsidiary of G-Resources Group Limited (1051.HK)

Funderstone Securities Holdings Limited’s securities and futures businesses have been in place since 1983 to serve private clients, institutions and investment advisors in the service provider region. It has its headquarters in Hong Kong. The experienced team of professionals serve clients all over the world.  Funderstone Group trades securities and futures contracts on behalf of its clients in all major markets around the world and has established fund management contacts in the region.
